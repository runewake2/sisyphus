## Summary

`sisyphus update <name> --priority high` changes a field without a state argument, and a closed
issue keeps its resolution and closed date when its state does not change.

## Changes

- `sisyphus update <name> [<state>]`: the state is optional. Without it, the state does not change,
  and at least one flag must be given.
- When the state does not change and `--resolution` is not given, the resolution and the closed
  date stay. Before, `update <name> closed --priority low` reset an abandoned issue to `completed`
  and its closed date to today.
- The warnings about closing an issue (open sub-issues, open dependents, an empty Resolution) come
  only when the issue closes, not when a closed issue gets another field change.
- `state` is optional in `sisyphus_update`.
- [[sisyphus-update]] describes the new form.
