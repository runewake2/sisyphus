# 0.0.20

- Date: 2026-10-08
- Bookmark: samw/ai/slug-ascii
- Components: cli, mcp

## Summary

`sisyphus slug` spells letters in ASCII and drops filler words before it keeps the first 6 words.
Its output is always ASCII.

## Changes

- Letters are decomposed (NFKD) and their marks removed, so `é` becomes `e`. Latin letters without
  a decomposition get an ASCII spelling: `ß` becomes `ss`, `æ` becomes `ae`, `ø` becomes `o`, and
  so on. A character with no ASCII form, such as a Cyrillic letter or an emoji, separates words.
- Filler words (`the`, `of`, `for`, and 17 more) drop before the 6-word cut, unless fewer than 2
  words would remain. `Build the issue index once per command` now gives
  `build-issue-index-once-per-command`, not `build-the-issue-index-once-per`.
- The help, the `sisyphus_slug` MCP description, and [[sisyphus-slug]] list the rules.
- `golang.org/x/text` is now a direct dependency, at the version already in the module graph.
