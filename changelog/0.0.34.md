## Summary

`sisyphus new` rejects a title that is empty or that has a line break or another control character.

## Changes

- `sisyphus new --title` fails for an empty title and for a title with a control character, for
  example `\n`, `\r`, or `\t`. Before, a line break split the `# Title` heading of the body over
  two lines and broke the `sisyphus list` table.
- [[sisyphus-new]] lists the rule.
- Tests cover `\n`, `\r`, `\t`, a blank title, and a title with non-ASCII text.
