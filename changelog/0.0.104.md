- Date: 2026-10-11
- Bookmark: samw/ai/ux-fixes

## Summary

The error for an invalid `--state` of `list` and `search` lists `all` among the valid values, and
suggests it for a near value such as `al`.

## Changes

- `list` and `search` check `--state` against the states and `all`. `update` and `new`, where `all`
  is not valid, do not change.
- A test covers the error of `list`, the suggestion of `search`, and the error of `update`.
